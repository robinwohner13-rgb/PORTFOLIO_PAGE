About Robin

A composer, pianist, and producer based in Copenhagen, Denmark.

Robin Wohner began playing classical piano at the age of seven. His participation in national piano competitions and concerts led to chamber music and music festival collaborations, laying the foundation for his work as a composer, pianist, and producer.

These classical roots remain central to his music, with particular inspiration drawn from Frédéric Chopin and Claude Debussy, alongside Japanese artists Joe Hisaishi, Ryuichi Sakamoto, and Ichiko Aoba. He develops his pieces independently from start to finish, composing the music, recording his own performances, and handling the programming and production in Logic Pro.

Beyond music, his interest in languages took him to Seoul, South Korea, where he lived on a working holiday visa and taught languages to children. During his time there, he discovered a passion for mathematics, which he now pursues through his studies in applied mathematics at Copenhagen Business School. Based in Copenhagen, he speaks Danish, English, Chinese, Japanese, and Korean.
