About Robin

A composer, pianist, and producer based in Copenhagen, Denmark.

Robin Wohner began playing classical piano at the age of seven. He has participated in a series of national piano competitions and concerts, which led to numerous chamber music and music festival collaborations.

Much of his music draws on his classical roots, particularly the works of Frédéric Chopin and Claude Debussy. He also finds inspiration in Japanese artists such as Joe Hisaishi, Ryuichi Sakamoto, and Ichiko Aoba.

He composes his own pieces, performs and records them himself, and handles the programming and production in Logic Pro, working independently from start to finish.

Alongside his work as a composer, pianist, and producer, he studies applied mathematics at Copenhagen Business School. He speaks five languages: Danish, English, Chinese, Japanese, and Korean.

He previously lived in Seoul, South Korea, on a working holiday visa, teaching languages to children. It was during his time in Korea that he discovered a passion for mathematics.
