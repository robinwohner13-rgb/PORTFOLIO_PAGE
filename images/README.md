# Images

Add your own images with these filenames:

- `landscape.jpg` — your misty hillside photograph, currently on the contact page
- `about.jpg` — portrait or performance photograph
- `film-01.jpg`, `film-02.jpg` — film stills or posters

The six release covers (`flower.jpg`, `nagori.jpg`, `dream-book.jpg`, `lycaenidae.jpg`, `hotaru.jpg`, `moon-ocean.jpg`) are already here. They came from Spotify's public oEmbed thumbnails for Robin's releases. If you use PNG or WebP for your photos, change the matching filename in the HTML and provide meaningful alt text.

`score-detail-01.png` and `score-detail-02.png` are your supplied sheet music images. The first appears as a tall rectangle on the music page, and the second on the home page.
