02 / A little about me

It started
at the piano.

I began playing classical piano at seven. It is still the place I return to.

Growing up in Denmark, national piano competitions and concerts led me to chamber music collaborations and music festivals. I continued my musical education at Det Kongelige Danske Musikkonservatorium and through the Jacob Gade talent programme.

My music grows from those classical roots: the poetry of Chopin, the colour of Debussy, and the worlds of Joe Hisaishi, Ryuichi Sakamoto and Ichiko Aoba. I compose my own pieces, record my performances, and program and produce each project in Logic Pro, from the first idea to the finished recording.

An interest in languages took me to Seoul, where I taught languages to children and discovered a passion for mathematics. Today, I am based in Copenhagen and study applied mathematics at Copenhagen Business School.

Robin Wohner
