Robin

Robin Wohner began studying classical piano as a child. Growing up particularly active in national piano competitions and youth concerts, he later pursued further musical education at Det Kongelige Danske Musikkonservatorium (RCM) and the talent acceleration program of Jacob Gade.

Robin lives in Copenhagen, Denmark.
