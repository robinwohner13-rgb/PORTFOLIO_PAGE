Composer / Pianist / Producer

Robin
Wohner

Robin Wohner began studying classical piano as a child in the Russian piano tradition. He later attended talent academies at DKDM while performing in competitions and concerts.

His performances include RCM Concert Hall, Tivoli Concert Hall and Aarhus Conservatory Concert Hall. Based in Copenhagen, he now composes and produces his own music.
